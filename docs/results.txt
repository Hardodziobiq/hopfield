TEST 1: learning rule performance and pattern stability
=======================================================================
A training pattern is stable if the network leaves every neuron
unchanged (sign(W·x) = x). Under Hebb, the local field combines
the signal (~1 - N/n) and crosstalk proportional to pattern similarity.
We compare Hebb and Oja on the supplied datasets.
